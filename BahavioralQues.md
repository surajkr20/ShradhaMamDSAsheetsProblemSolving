
## 🟢 **1. Introductory Questions (Must-Practice)**

These set the tone. Practice them daily in front of a mirror:

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Walk me through your resume.
3. Why do you want to work with our company?
4. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
5. Why should we hire you?
6. What do you know about our company?
7. Why did you choose Computer Applications / IT / Software field?
8. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
9. What is your dream company and why?
10. What makes you different from other candidates?

---

## 🟡 **2. HR Behavioral Questions**

These check your soft skills, mindset, and work ethics:

11. Tell me about a time when you worked in a team.
12. Have you ever faced conflict in a team? How did you handle it?
13. Tell me about a time you failed. What did you learn?
14. Describe a challenging situation and how you overcame it.
15. How do you handle pressure and deadlines?
16. How do you deal with negative feedback?
17. Are you open to relocation or working remotely?
18. Are you comfortable with learning new technologies quickly?
19. What motivates you every day?
20. How do you manage your time and prioritize tasks?

---

## 🟠 **3. Situation/Scenario-Based Questions**

These test your decision-making and logical thinking in real-life cases:

21. If you’re given a task you don’t know how to do, what will you do?
22. If your team member is not cooperating, how will you handle it?
23. What would you do if your manager asks you to do something unethical?
24. You have two tasks with the same deadline. How will you prioritize?
25. What would you do if your project deadline is tomorrow and your system crashes today?

---

## 🔵 **4. Self-Awareness + Personality Questions**

These help interviewers understand your mindset:

26. What is your biggest achievement so far?
27. What motivates you to keep learning?
28. What does success mean to you?
29. What kind of work environment do you prefer?
30. What are your hobbies and how do they help you?
31. What is your biggest fear?
32. Who is your role model and why?
33. How do you define leadership?
34. How do you stay updated in tech?
35. How do you handle boredom and distractions?

---

## 🔥 BONUS: Technical + English Crossover

36. Explain your project in **simple English to a non-technical person**.
37. What was your role in the project? What was the biggest challenge?
38. Tell me about a bug you fixed recently.
39. How do you stay calm when stuck in debugging?
40. What’s one recent tech topic you learned and liked?

---

## 📌 Tips for Practice:

* Practice **daily 2–3 questions aloud**.
* Record and listen to yourself.
* Use **simple, clear English** — don’t aim for fancy words.
* Use **STAR format** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for story-based answers.
